# CAMPOZY OPERATIONS FRAMEWORK

## Status
Operational constitution.

## Core Philosophy
Operations sustain trust.

## Operations Hierarchy
1. Student Protection
2. Trust Protection
3. Data Integrity
4. Platform Growth
5. Revenue Operations

## Core Operational Units
- Trust & Safety
- Verification Operations
- Scout Operations
- Community Operations
- Support Operations
- Growth Operations
- Data & Intelligence Operations
- Business Operations

## Trust & Safety Operations
Queues:
- urgent safety
- fraud
- property abuse
- messaging abuse
- review manipulation
- appeals

## Case Priority System
- Critical: within 1 hour
- High: within 12 hours
- Standard: within 48 hours
- Routine: within 7 days

## Verification Operations
Flow:
Request → Evidence → Automated Checks → Human Review → Decision → Audit Trail

## Scout Operations
Lifecycle:
Recruit → Train → Shadow → Certified Scout → Senior Scout → Lead Scout

## Scout Training
- platform principles
- evidence collection
- safety procedures
- verification standards
- ethics
- report writing
- fraud detection

## Community Operations
Goal:
promote useful contributions and reduce misinformation and toxicity.

## Support Operations
Categories:
- account issues

- opportunity issues

## Growth Operations
Own:
- campus launches
- ambassador programs

## Data & Intelligence Operations

- trust analytics
- fraud analytics
- campus intelligence
- growth analytics
- opportunity analytics

## Business Operations
Own:
- owner relationships
- business relationships
- employer partnerships
- university partnerships
- revenue operations

Business Operations must never control:
- trust scores
- verification decisions
- reputation scores
- recommendation outputs

## Incident Management
Incident lifecycle:
Detection → Classification → Investigation → Response → Resolution → Postmortem

## Operational Principles
- document everything
- create audit trails
- measure outcomes
- protect students
- preserve trust
- scale carefully

## Operations North Star
Every operational decision should improve trust, safety, confidence, or decision quality.
