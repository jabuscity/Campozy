# CAMPOZY CONTENT STRATEGY

## Status
Content growth constitution.

## Core Philosophy
Content is infrastructure. Campozys strongest growth content is captured knowledge, not marketing copy.

## Content Mission
Help students make better decisions.

## Content Hierarchy
1. Decision Content
2. Trust Content
3. Community Content
4. Opportunity Content
5. Brand Content

## Content Flywheel
Student searches
→ finds content
→ gets value
→ joins Campozy
→ contributes knowledge
→ content expands
→ visibility improves
→ more students arrive

## Content Pillars
- Housing Intelligence
- Campus Intelligence
- Neighborhood Intelligence
- Student Life
- Opportunities
- Community Knowledge

- overview

## GEO Strategy
Support AI-native discovery through:
- structured data
- trust signals
- entity relationships
- author attribution

## Content Quality Framework
Content must be:
- useful
- accurate
- trustworthy
- current
- relevant
- student-centered

## Metrics
- search traffic
- contribution rate
- review coverage
- discussion coverage
- opportunity engagement
- time to first value

## Content North Star
Every article, review, discussion, and report should help a student make a better decision than they could have made without Campozy.
