# CAMPOZY RECOMMENDATION ENGINE

## Status
Strategic intelligence document.

## Purpose
Help students make better decisions, not merely faster ones.

## Core Principles
- Recommendations are not advertisements
- Trust comes before personalization
- Recommendations must be explainable
- Behavior matters more than claims
- Recommendations improve continuously

## Recommendation Layers
1. Rule-Based Recommendations
2. Preference Engine
3. Behavior Engine
4. Student Similarity Engine
5. Property Similarity Engine
6. Neighborhood Intelligence Engine
7. Utility Recommendation Engine
8. Hygiene Recommendation Engine
9. Trust Engine
10. Knowledge Graph Engine
11. Lifecycle Recommendation Engine
12. Opportunity Engine
13. Mentorship Engine
14. Business Recommendation Engine
15. Akwet Transition Engine

## Inputs
- identity signals
- behavior signals
- trust signals
- community signals
- lifecycle signals

## Campozy Score Integration
Recommendations incorporate:
- Safety
- Hygiene & Sanitation
- Water Reliability
- Electricity Reliability
- Internet Reliability
- Management Quality
- Accessibility
- Value For Money
- Community Sentiment
- Verification Confidence

## Recommendation Confidence
Every recommendation has a confidence level and should be explainable.

## Explainability
Example:
Recommended because:
- students like you preferred it
- hygiene score is strong
- WiFi reliability is high
- the property is verified
- it is near your campus

## Negative Intelligence
The system should also help users avoid poor decisions by surfacing warning signals.

## Success Metrics
- recommendation acceptance
- saves
- inquiries
- satisfaction
- opportunity applications
- mentorship participation

## Strategic Outcome
Every interaction improves future recommendations and every recommendation strengthens the Trust Graph.
