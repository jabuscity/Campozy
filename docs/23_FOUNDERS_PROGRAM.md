# CAMPOZY FOUNDERS PROGRAM v3

## Status
Founders constitution.

## Core Philosophy
Founders are not early signups.
Founders are builders.

## Founder Mission
Create a generation of student leaders who help build:
- housing intelligence
- trust intelligence
- campus intelligence
- opportunity intelligence
- community intelligence

## Founding Principles
- Contribute before consuming
- Help students
- Protect trust
- Improve information quality
- Strengthen local communities
- Build for future students

## Founding Membership Architecture
Campozy recognizes founders at three levels:

Campus  
↓  
Country  
↓  
Global

## Campus Founders
Examples:
- Founding 50 UON
- Founding 50 JKUAT
- Founding 50 KU
- Founding 50 Makerere

Campus founder slots are limited and earned by qualified contributors.

## Country Founders
Examples:
- Founding 100 Kenya
- Founding 100 Nigeria
- Founding 100 Uganda
- Founding 100 Ghana

Every country receives its own founder cohorts.

## Global Founders
Examples:
- Global Pioneer 100
- Global Pioneer 500
- Global Pioneer 1000
- Continental Builder

## Eligibility Philosophy
Founder status:
- cannot be purchased
- cannot be gifted
- cannot be inherited
- cannot be earned through registration alone

## Qualified Contributor Requirement
Founder slots lock only after a user becomes a qualified contributor.

Requirements may include:
- account creation
- identity verification
- onboarding completion
- contribution activity
- good standing
- minimum trust threshold

## Qualifying Contributions
- property reviews
- utility reports
- hygiene reports
- neighborhood intelligence
- discussion contributions
- campus guides
- business reviews
- verification evidence
- opportunity contributions
- mentorship
- community leadership
- referrals

## Founder Contribution Score
Inputs:
- contribution volume
- contribution quality
- consistency
- trust score
- impact
- verification participation
- leadership activity
- referral activity

## Founder Identity System
A founder may hold multiple identities:
- Campus Founder
- Country Founder
- Global Pioneer

## Founder Benefits
- permanent founder badge
- permanent recognition
- founder directory
- private founder community
- roadmap visibility
- early feature access
- feedback channels
- leadership opportunities
- pilot-program access

## Founder Opportunity Program
Examples:
- internship alerts
- fellowship alerts
- scholarship alerts
- pilot invitations
- leadership programs
- mentorship matching

Priority visibility is allowed.
Guaranteed outcomes are not.

## Founder Progression Model
User
→ Contributor
→ Campus Founder
→ Country Founder
→ Global Pioneer
→ Campus Expert
→ Ambassador
→ Scout
→ Mentor
→ Alumni Leader
→ Employer Partner

## Founders and Ambassadors
Many ambassadors should emerge from the founder base.

## Founders and Scouts
Many scouts should emerge from trusted contributors and founders.

## Founders and Alumni
Founder status survives graduation.

## Founders and Akwet
Founders may continue into the Akwet ecosystem as professionals, employers, or housing partners.

## Country Expansion Policy
When Campozy launches in a new country, new founder cohorts are created there.
Founder opportunities do not disappear because another country launched earlier.

## Founder Responsibilities
Founders are expected to:
- lead by example
- protect trust
- promote accuracy
- support new members
- report abuse
- improve information quality
- strengthen community health
- encourage contributions

## Governance
Founders may participate in:
- founder surveys
- feedback panels
- roadmap consultations
- community advisory sessions

Founders advise. Operations decide.

## Founder Misconduct
Founder status does not create immunity.
Serious violations may result in restrictions, suspensions, badge removal, or permanent removal.

## Founder Metrics
Track:
- contribution quality
- contribution volume
- community impact
- referral activity
- leadership activity
- mentorship activity
- trust scores
- retention

## Founders Program North Star
A founder should be able to say:
- I helped build Campozy in my campus.
- I helped build Campozy in my country.
- I helped build Campozy across Africa.
