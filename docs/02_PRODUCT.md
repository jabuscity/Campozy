# CAMPOZY PRODUCT

## Status
Core product definition.

## Product Summary
Campozy is a Student Trust Network designed to help students make better housing and life decisions. Housing is the entry point; student success is the destination.

## Product Mission
Provide students with trusted information, verified intelligence, community knowledge, and opportunity access throughout their academic journey and beyond.

## Core Problems
Students struggle to answer:
- Is this place safe?
- Is water reliable?
- Is electricity reliable?
- Is WiFi usable?
- Is management cooperative?
- Is this neighborhood worth the price?
- What do real students think?

## Users
Primary:
- Students

Secondary:
- Parents
- Property owners
- Scouts
- Alumni
- Employers
- Local businesses

## Product Pillars
### 1. Housing Intelligence
Property discovery, comparison, reviews, utility tracking, neighborhood intelligence.

### 2. Trust & Reputation
Trust scores, contribution scores, badges, authority signals.

### 3. Verification
Property, owner, student, scout, business, and employer verification.

### 4. Community Intelligence
Questions, answers, discussions, guides, tips, warnings.

### 5. Recommendation Engine
Properties, neighborhoods, businesses, mentors, opportunities, Akwet transitions.

### 6. Student Lifecycle Platform
Prospective student → student → contributor → founder → ambassador/scout → graduate → alumni → mentor/employer.

### 7. Local Business Ecosystem
Student-serving businesses with reviews, trust signals, and recommendations.

### 8. Parent Confidence Layer
Safety, utilities, trust, and housing confidence reporting.

### 9. Opportunity Network
Internships, scholarships, fellowships, mentorship, ambassador programs.

### 10. Graduate Transition Layer
Akwet integration and professional-housing transition.

### 11. Growth Layer
Referrals, founders, ambassadors, scouts, community recognition.

### 12. Matching Layer
Roommate Finder and Friendfinder — trust-based compatibility matching for students seeking housing partners and social connections.
