# CAMPOZY VERIFICATION SYSTEM

## Status
Trust infrastructure document.

## Purpose
Verification answers: can this information be trusted?

Verification does not answer: is this information good?

## Core Principles
- Trust must be earned
- Verification is layered
- Evidence matters
- Multiple independent sources increase confidence
- Verification must be transparent

## Verification Levels
- Unverified
- Claimed
- Community Verified
- Scout Verified
- Campozy Verified

## Verification Confidence
Generated from:
- evidence quality
- source diversity
- consistency
- recency
- historical reliability

## Property Verification
Targets:
- existence
- location
- pricing
- amenities
- utilities
- management details
- availability

## Owner Verification
Targets:
- identity
- contact information
- property association
- business legitimacy

## Student Verification
Targets:
- contact verification
- student affiliation
- contribution history
- community validation

## Review Verification
Factors:
- reviewer reputation
- evidence
- consistency
- community validation

## Utility Verification
Water, electricity, internet, security, accessibility all have verification confidence.

## Hygiene Verification
Bathrooms, kitchens, waste management, pests, sanitation and common-area conditions are first-class verification domains.

## Business and Employer Verification
Required to protect the opportunity and local-business ecosystems.

## Scout Verification
Scouts themselves must be verified, trained, and auditable.

## Community Verification
Users can confirm, dispute, or add evidence to strengthen confidence.

## Fraud Detection
Detect:
- fake listings
- fake reviews
- fake businesses
- verification fraud
- opportunity fraud
- vote manipulation

## Verification & Campozy Score
Verification influences confidence, not quality. A poor property can still be highly verified.
