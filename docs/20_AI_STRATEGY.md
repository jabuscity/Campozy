# CAMPOZY AI STRATEGY

## Status
AI constitution.

## Core Principle
Data
→ Trust
→ Graphs
→ Intelligence
→ AI
→ Better Decisions

AI comes last, not first.

## AI Mission
Use AI to help students make better decisions.
Never use AI to replace judgment or human accountability.

## What AI Is Not
AI is not:
- the product
- the moat
- the trust layer
- the verification layer
- the reputation layer

## AI Deployment Timeline
Phase 0: No AI  
Phase 1: Rule-Based Intelligence  
Phase 2: Behavior Intelligence  
Phase 3: Graph Intelligence  
Phase 4: AI-Assisted Intelligence  
Phase 5: Advanced AI Features

## Human-In-The-Loop
Mandatory human review for:
- verification disputes
- account bans
- appeals
- trust investigations
- fraud investigations
- delistings
- safety incidents

## Allowed AI Functions
- recommendation explanations
- review summaries
- discussion summaries
- trend analysis
- search enhancement
- knowledge extraction
- anomaly detection

## Prohibited AI Autonomy
AI may not autonomously:
- ban users
- remove accounts
- verify properties
- assign trust alone
- decide appeals
- invent evidence
- fabricate reviews or reports

## Explainability Requirement
Every AI-assisted recommendation should explain why it appeared.

## AI Governance
- transparency
- accountability
- auditability
- privacy
- human oversight
- contestability
- safety

## Audit Trails
Log:
- input
- output
- confidence
- timestamp
- model version
- human override

## AI Success Metrics
Measure:
- decision confidence
- recommendation accuracy
- time to value
- trust retention
- user satisfaction
- student outcomes

## AI North Star
AI is successful when it quietly improves decisions without becoming the center of attention.
