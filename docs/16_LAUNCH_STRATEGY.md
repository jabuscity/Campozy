# CAMPOZY LAUNCH STRATEGY v2

## Status
Launch constitution.

## Core Launch Philosophy
Do not launch to Africa.  
Do not launch to Kenya.  
Launch to one campus.

## Launch Sequence
- Private Validation
- Campus Launch
- University Expansion
- City Expansion
- Country Expansion
- Regional Expansion
- Continental Expansion

## Phase 0: Private Validation
Goal: prove students will contribute information.

## Phase 1: Campus Launch
Goal: achieve campus liquidity.

Campus checklist:
- property coverage
- review coverage
- utility coverage
- hygiene coverage
- discussion activity
- founder participation

## Campus Founder Program
Examples:
- Founding 50 UON
- Founding 50 JKUAT
- Founding 50 KU

Founder slots are earned through contribution, trust, participation, and impact.

## Ambassador Launch Program
Initial target: 1 ambassador per campus.

## Scout Launch Program
Initial target: 5–20 scouts per major campus.

## Community Launch Strategy
Create dense local interaction:
- questions
- answers
- reviews
- reports
- discussions

## Launch Flywheel
Student needs housing
→ discovers Campozy
→ gets value
→ contributes information
→ platform improves
→ future students get more value
→ more students join

## University Expansion
Campus density first, then full university ownership.

## Country Founders
Examples:
- Founding 100 Kenya
- Founding 100 Nigeria
- Founding 100 Uganda
- Founding 100 Ghana

## Property Owner Strategy
Students first. Owners later.

Sequence:
Students → Trust → Reviews → Traffic → Owners → Listings

## SEO Launch Strategy
Launch pages:
- university pages
- campus pages
- neighborhood pages
- property pages
- discussion pages
- business pages
- opportunity pages

## Expansion Rule
Never expand because you can. Expand because liquidity exists.

## Launch Metrics
- review coverage
- discussion coverage
- contribution rate
- campus density
- founder growth
- ambassador growth
- scout growth
- liquidity
- trust signals

## Launch North Star
When students become contributors, contributors become founders, founders become leaders, and leaders create future contributors, the launch has succeeded.
