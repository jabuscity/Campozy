# CAMPOZY OPPORTUNITY ENGINE

## Status
Strategic expansion document.

## Purpose
Transform Campozy from a housing platform into a long-term student success ecosystem.

## Opportunity Types
- internships
- attachments
- scholarships
- fellowships
- competitions
- graduate trainee programs
- mentorship
- ambassador programs
- employer partnerships

## Core Principles
- Opportunity is a trust product
- Student success matters
- Trust before opportunity
- Transparency matters
- Opportunities are discovered and earned

## Matching Inputs
- program
- university
- year of study
- interests
- skills
- reputation
- contribution history
- lifecycle stage

## Engines
- Internship Engine
- Scholarship Engine
- Fellowship Engine
- Competition Engine
- Mentorship Engine
- Alumni Engine
- Ambassador Engine
- Employer Partnership Engine

## Founders and Opportunities
Qualified founders may get priority visibility for alerts and early-access programs, but not guaranteed outcomes.

## Verification
All opportunities, employers, and mentors should have verification flows.

## Opportunity Ethics
No employer may buy trust or recommendation priority.

## Long-Term Vision
Student → Opportunity → Success → Alumni → Mentor/Employer → New Opportunity for future students
