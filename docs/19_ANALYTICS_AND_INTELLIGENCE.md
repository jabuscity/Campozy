# CAMPOZY ANALYTICS & INTELLIGENCE FRAMEWORK

## Status
Measurement constitution.

## Core Philosophy
Good analytics create clarity. Campozys metrics should prioritize trust, liquidity, contribution, and decision quality before vanity metrics.

## Analytics Hierarchy
1. Trust
2. Liquidity
3. Contribution
4. Decision Quality
5. Growth
6. Revenue

## North Star Metric
Student Decision Confidence

Proxy signals:
- review coverage
- verification coverage
- utility coverage
- hygiene coverage
- recommendation acceptance
- student satisfaction

## Intelligence Systems
- Trust Intelligence
- Housing Intelligence
- Opportunity Intelligence
- Growth Intelligence

## Trust Intelligence Metrics
- verification coverage
- review confidence
- trust score distribution
- verified property ratio
- fraud detection rate
- appeal success rate

## Liquidity Intelligence Metrics
- property coverage
- review coverage
- utility coverage
- hygiene coverage
- campus coverage
- response times

## Contribution Intelligence Metrics
- reviews submitted
- utility reports
- hygiene reports
- discussions created
- answers posted
- verification evidence
- referrals

## Community Intelligence
Measure:
- participation
- answer rate
- helpful-vote rate
- report rate
- contributor retention

## Housing Intelligence
Property, neighborhood, and campus intelligence should capture:
- demand
- pricing
- utilities
- safety
- sentiment
- scores

## Recommendation Intelligence
Measure:
- acceptance rate
- save rate
- dismissal rate
- confidence
- accuracy

## Opportunity Intelligence
Measure:
- applications
- mentorship matches
- opportunity acceptance
- employer participation
- graduate outcomes

## Alumni Intelligence
Measure:
- graduates retained
- mentorship participation
- opportunity sharing
- alumni contributions

## Campus Intelligence
Measure:
- student satisfaction
- housing reliability
- utility reliability
- safety trends
- opportunity density
- business quality

## Dashboards

- Opportunity Dashboard

## Anti-Patterns
Do not optimize for:
- page views
- downloads
- vanity followers
- empty engagement

## Governance
Analytics must remain transparent, explainable, auditable, and privacy-aware.

## North Star
Analytics should help students make better decisions than they could have made without Campozy.
