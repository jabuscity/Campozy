# CAMPOZY REPUTATION SYSTEM

## Status
Trust infrastructure document.

## Purpose
Reputation identifies credibility, not popularity.

## Core Principles
- Trust must be earned
- Quality beats quantity
- Context matters
- Transparency matters
- Reputation can decline

## Reputation Entities
- students
- property owners
- properties
- scouts
- businesses
- mentors
- alumni
- ambassadors

## Components
- Reputation Score
- Trust Score
- Contribution Score
- Confidence Score
- Verification Status

## Student Reputation
Positive signals:
- helpful reviews
- accurate reports
- discussions
- utility reports
- hygiene reports
- mentorship
- opportunity contributions

Negative signals:
- spam
- fake reviews
- manipulation
- harassment
- repeated misleading reports

## Reputation Levels
- Member
- Contributor
- Trusted Contributor
- Campus Expert
- Community Leader
- Campozy Fellow

## Owner Reputation
Measured by response quality, complaints, transparency, verification participation, and maintenance behavior.

## Scout Reputation
Measured by verification accuracy, evidence quality, and audit outcomes.

## Business Reputation
Measured by reviews, consistency, complaints, and trust signals.

## Mentor and Alumni Reputation
Measured by mentorship quality, contribution consistency, leadership, and opportunity sharing.

## Review Weighting
Not all reviews carry equal influence. High-trust contributors and verified evidence raise weight.

## Badges
Examples:
- Trusted Reviewer
- Campus Expert
- Utility Expert
- Hygiene Expert
- Mentor
- Community Leader

## Founders and Reputation
Founder recognition does not automatically create trust; contribution and integrity do.

## Anti-Manipulation
Detect:
- review rings
- reciprocal voting
- reputation farming
- brigading
- fake accounts

## End State
A system where contribution quality, credibility, and trustworthiness are visible and hard to fake.
