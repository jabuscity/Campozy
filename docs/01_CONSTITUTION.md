# CAMPOZY CONSTITUTION

## Status
Foundational governing document.

## Founding Premise
Campozy is not a hostel listing site.  
Campozy is a Student Trust Network.

Housing is the entry point.  
Trust is the product.  
Intelligence is the moat.  
Opportunity is the long-term expansion layer.

## Vision
To become Africa's most trusted student intelligence network.

## Mission
Help students make better living decisions through trusted housing intelligence, community knowledge, verification systems, reputation systems, and opportunity networks.

## Long-Term Evolution
Student Housing Intelligence  
→ Student Living Platform  
→ Student Trust Graph  
→ Student Opportunity Network  
→ Africa's Student Intelligence Network

## Core Pillars
1. Housing Intelligence
2. Trust & Reputation
3. Verification
4. Community Knowledge
5. Recommendation Intelligence
6. Student Lifecycle Support
7. Local Business Ecosystem
8. Parent Confidence Layer
9. Opportunity Network
10. Graduate Transition Layer
11. Growth & Referral Layer

## Core Principles
- Trust above growth
- Trust above revenue
- Every visit creates value
- No wasted visits
- Progressive profiling
- Infrastructure before activation
- Community before content
- Transparency builds trust

## What Campozy Is
- a student intelligence platform
- a housing intelligence platform
- a trust platform
- a recommendation platform
- a community knowledge platform
- an opportunity platform

## What Campozy Is Not
- a classifieds site
- a generic real-estate portal
- a booking engine
- a social network clone

## Student Trust Graph
The Student Trust Graph combines:
- identity
- behavior
- reputation
- verification
- housing intelligence
- community intelligence
- opportunity intelligence

## Campozy Score Philosophy
The Campozy Score exists to support decisions and cannot be purchased.

Scoring dimensions:
- Safety
- Hygiene & Sanitation
- Water Reliability
- Electricity Reliability
- Internet Reliability
- Management Quality
- Accessibility
- Value For Money
- Community Sentiment
- Verification Confidence

## Revenue Philosophy
Revenue should strengthen the ecosystem, not weaken it.

## End State
Campozy becomes the default place students trust before making major living decisions and eventually evolves into a lifelong student and alumni intelligence network.
