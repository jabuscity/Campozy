# CAMPOZY GROWTH ENGINE v2

## Status
Growth constitution.

## Core Philosophy
Growth is the natural consequence of trust, contribution, intelligence, and community.

## North Star
The goal is not more users.  
The goal is more useful decisions.

## Core Flywheels
### Campozy Flywheel
Student needs housing  
→ finds Campozy  
→ finds value  
→ contributes information  
→ platform improves  
→ future students receive more value  
→ more students arrive

### Intelligence Flywheel
Reviews  
→ trust  
→ recommendations  
→ better decisions  
→ more reviews

### Community Flywheel
Questions  
→ answers  
→ trust  
→ more contributors  
→ more knowledge

### Founder Flywheel
Contributor  
→ Campus Founder  
→ Country Founder  
→ Global Pioneer  
→ Community Leader  
→ Mentor  
→ Alumni  
→ Employer  
→ Opportunity Provider

## Campus Growth Engine
Expansion order:
Campus → University → City → Country → Region → Africa

## Campus Launch Model
1. Acquire contributors
2. Acquire reviews
3. Acquire discussions
4. Acquire utility reports
5. Acquire founders
6. Launch ambassador program
7. Launch scout program
8. Expand

## Founder Growth Engine
Founders create ownership, recruitment, leadership, validation, and local trust.

## Ambassador Growth Engine
Ambassadors recruit contributors, identify founders, promote discussions, and build density.

## Scout Growth Engine
Scouts accelerate trust through verification, audits, and intelligence gathering.

## Referral Engine
Referral types:
- student
- founder
- ambassador
- scout
- alumni
- employer

## Content Growth Engine
Reviews, discussions, campus guides, neighborhood guides, and opportunity pages become acquisition assets.

## Opportunity Growth Engine
Student → opportunity → success → alumni → mentor/employer → new opportunities

## Alumni Growth Engine
Student → graduate → alumni → mentor → employer → partner

## Geographic Expansion Rule
Never expand because you can. Expand because liquidity exists.

## Key Metrics
- contribution rate
- referral rate
- founder growth
- campus coverage
- discussion density
- review density
- verification density
- opportunity participation
- alumni participation

## Growth Principles
- Trust before scale
- Density before geography
- Contributors before consumers
- Community before marketing
- Retention before acquisition
- Intelligence before monetization

## Growth North Star
When students become contributors, contributors become founders, founders become leaders, and leaders create future contributors, growth becomes self-sustaining.
