# CAMPOZY MONETIZATION STRATEGY

## Status
Revenue constitution.

## Core Philosophy
Trust is the product. Revenue is a consequence of trust.

## Correct Order
Trust → Liquidity → Network Effects → Growth → Monetization → Expansion

## Golden Rule
Campozy never sells:
- trust
- verification outcomes
- reputation
- recommendation rankings
- Campozy scores

## Revenue Sources
1. Property owners
2. Businesses
3. Employers
4. Premium intelligence
5. Advertising
6. Premium convenience
7. Future services

## Property Owner Revenue
- featured placement
- enhanced analytics
- lead intelligence
- portfolio insights

Featured placement never changes trust or recommendations.

## Verification Revenue
Users may pay for verification review, not verification approval.

## Business Revenue
- verification
- featured visibility
- business analytics
- promotional campaigns

## Employer Revenue
- opportunity listings
- verified employer profiles
- employer branding
- recruitment analytics
- talent discovery

## Premium Intelligence
Sell insights, not access:
- neighborhood trends
- housing intelligence
- utility trends
- student sentiment
- campus intelligence

## Advertising Rules
Allowed:
- education
- internet services
- banking
- insurance
- scholarships
- career services

Not allowed:
- scams
- predatory lending
- gambling
- misleading offers

## Student Premium
May include:
- advanced alerts
- saved-search automation
- enhanced comparison tools
- early feature access

Core trust and housing intelligence must remain accessible.

## Lifecycle Monetization
Campozy → Graduate → Akwet → Professional Housing

## Revenue Principles
- Charge organizations more
- Charge students less
- Never compromise trust
- Separate revenue from trust systems

## North Star
If a revenue idea weakens trust, reject it.
