# CAMPOZY TRUST & SAFETY FRAMEWORK

## Status
Platform protection constitution.

## Core Philosophy
Safety by design, not safety by reaction.

## Priority Order
1. Student safety
2. Student trust
3. Platform integrity
4. Community health
5. Business interests
6. Revenue

## Safety Pillars
1. Identity Trust
2. Property Trust
3. Community Safety
4. Communication Safety
5. Information Integrity
6. Fraud Prevention

## Identity Trust
Protect against:
- fake accounts
- impersonation
- identity fraud
- account farming

## Property Trust
Signals:
- verification
- review volume
- review confidence
- utility confidence
- hygiene confidence
- owner reputation

## Community Safety
Allowed:
- questions
- answers
- reviews
- advice
- warnings
- constructive criticism

Not allowed:
- harassment
- bullying
- threats
- doxxing
- hate speech
- incitement
- spam
- misinformation

## Messaging Safety
Tools:
- report
- block
- mute
- leave conversation
- trust-based messaging limits

## Student Protection
Extra protection for new students, first-time renters, and vulnerable users.

## Scout Protection
Scouts must operate with safety protocols and auditable evidence standards.

## Fraud Prevention
Categories:
- property fraud
- identity fraud
- review fraud
- business fraud
- opportunity fraud
- referral fraud

## Review Integrity
Forbidden:
- fake reviews
- review rings
- self-reviews
- extortion
- competitor attacks

## Moderation Layers
1. automated detection
2. community reporting
3. moderator review
4. appeal review

## Enforcement
- educational warning
- content removal
- temporary restriction
- suspension
- permanent ban

## Appeals
Every major enforcement action should be appealable.

## Transparency
Users should know:
- why content was removed
- why restrictions happened
- why verification failed
- why recommendations changed

## Trust Metrics
- report volume
- resolution time
- fraud detection rate
- false positive rate
- appeal success rate
- review confidence coverage
- verification coverage

## North Star
Students should be able to say:
- I trust the information
- I trust the people
- I trust the recommendations
- I trust the platform to protect me
